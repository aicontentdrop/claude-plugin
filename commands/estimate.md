---
description: Work out what a video or image job, or a batch of them, will cost in credits and whether a stated balance covers it
argument-hint: <model and quantity, or a shot list; optionally the current balance>
---

Apply the bundled `credits-and-plans` skill to: $ARGUMENTS

If the text after the colon is empty or a placeholder, apply it to the person's latest request. Work as an advisor: do not generate anything, run shell commands or scripts of any kind, sign anyone in or ask for account details. Everything you produce is text.

1. Read the prices from the live catalogue: https://aicontentdrop.com/v1/models?type=video for video and https://aicontentdrop.com/v1/models?type=image for images. Price several models from the same read. If you cannot read it, say so and do not guess a price.
2. Compute credits needed = quantity times credits each, per model and in total. Say when a row needs a start image: a row marked `needs_start_image` only animates a still the person supplies. Most video models cost one flat price per generation. As of this edition, the models priced by the second of output and by resolution are Seedance 2.5 (`seedance_2_5`), Seedance 2.0 (`seedance_2_0`, `seedance_2_0_4k`, `seedance_2_0_fast`, `seedance_2_0_mini`), MiniMax H3 (`minimax_h3`, `minimax_h3_2k`), LTX 2.5 (`ltx_2_5_fast`, `ltx_2_5_pro`), FLUX 3 video (`flux_3_video`) and Wan 3.0 (`wan_3_0`). Seedance 1.x models are flat, and so are `seedance_2_0_multishot` and `seedance_2_fast`. Name these models by id or full name, never as a family. For a per-second model, say that the catalogue row is the price at the default length (10 seconds at 1080p for Wan 3.0), that a longer or sharper take costs more, and that the card on aicontentdrop.com shows the exact figure before anything is made. Do not estimate how much more; the card shows the figure. If the person gave a balance, state balance, cost and shortfall as three numbers, but only for a job the catalogue row prices; for a per-second model at a length or resolution other than its default, give the row as the default-length price only: do not multiply it by the quantity, compare it with the balance or print a shortfall, not even as a reference.
3. If it does not fit, name the cheaper model, the smaller batch or the draft-then-final split that does. If the person wants a per-second model at a length or resolution other than its default, its catalogue row is not the price, so never count a job or an option that includes that model as fitting a balance; say that the card on aicontentdrop.com shows the exact figure before anything is made. A draft-then-final split whose final model is priced by the second at a non-default length fits only for its drafts, and say so in the same sentence as the split; the cost of the finals is on the card. Credits for a video or image are charged only when it succeeds; failures cost nothing.
4. If the person asks what a plan includes, send them to https://aicontentdrop.com/plans and say nothing more about plans here.
5. Finish with one link that describes the job, URL-encoded with spaces as %20: `https://aicontentdrop.com/chat#prompt=<url-encoded description>`. Say what opening it does: a signed-out visitor is asked to sign in first; on a signed-in account the chat on aicontentdrop.com sends the description at once; a chat reply costs 1 to 3 credits; and a video or image is made only when the person presses the card that shows the model and its credit cost.
